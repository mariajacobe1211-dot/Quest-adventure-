Q1. What should a responsible driver do when the traffic light turns yellow?

  • A. Sabihin “kaya pa ‘to!” habang dinidiinan ang gas
  • B. Magdasal habang tumatawid sa intersection
  • C. Slow down and prepare to stop
  • D. Bilisan kasi baka maunahan ka ng red light

Answer: C
