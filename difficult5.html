Q5. What should you do during an earthquake while you are inside a building?

  • A. Sumigaw ng “MAMA!” habang naghahanap ng matatakbuhan 😂
  • B. Run outside immediately kahit may nahuhulog na bagay
  • C. Drop, Cover, and Hold On until the shaking stops and pray for God’s protection 🙂
  • D. Mag‑video muna para may emergency content

Answer: C
