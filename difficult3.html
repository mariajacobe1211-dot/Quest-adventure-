Q3. What is the maximum speed limit on a national highway (when conditions are safe and no lower speed limit is posted)?

  • A. 120 km/h — "Maluwag naman ang daan, kaya pa yan!" 😎
  • B. 80 km/h — "Safe na yan, hindi naman ako nagmamadali."
  • C. 100 km/h — "Hindi pa naman lumilipad ang kotse, sakto lang!" 😂
  • D. 150 km/h — "Testing lang kung gaano kalakas makina."

Answer: C — 100 km/h
