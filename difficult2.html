Q2. What does a solid white line on the road usually mean?

  • A. Pwede lumipat ng lane basta mabilis ang kotse
  • B. Road decoration lang para hindi boring tingnan
  • C. Avoid crossing or changing lanes unless permitted because it separates traffic lanes
